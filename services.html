Services by Category

End-to-end manufacturing capabilities—from machining and casting to inspection, supply, and technical guidance.

Precision Machining

Custom and production machining of components to exact client specifications, including turning, milling, drilling, and threading on stainless steel, brass, and alloy materials.

Casting Solutions

Stainless steel (SS316L, 304L) casting with precision die moulds, covering design, casting, and finishing, tailored to each application's requirements.

Fabrication & Assembly

Custom fabrication of pipe fittings, flanges, brackets, and support components, assembled and quality checked before dispatch.

Quality Testing & Inspection

Components undergo radiography testing and dimensional inspection to ensure every part meets required specifications before it leaves our facility.

Product Sourcing & Supply

We supply a wide range of stainless steel components, fittings, and industrial hardware, sourced or manufactured to match project specific needs.

Technical Consultation

Our team works with clients from the requirement stage through to final delivery, helping identify the right materials, tolerances, and manufacturing approach for each project.
