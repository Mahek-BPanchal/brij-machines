## Skid Steer Attachments

Custom design, development, and manufacturing of skid steer attachments, built as per client requirement and also manufactured for OEM industries.

### Backhoe Attachment

A skid steer-mounted backhoe attachment for excavation and digging applications.

**Key Features:**
- Adjustable control section
- Compatible with all major skid steer models
- Independent stabilizer for stability
- Smooth operation hydraulic cylinders with cushioning technology
- Higher breakout force at dipper and bucket
- Side shifting feature

**Technical Specification:**
- Operating Weight: 642 kg
- Frame Width: 1640 mm
- Max. Swing Angle: 174°
- Max. Digging Depth: 2640 mm
- Max. Oil Flow (LPM): 90 ltr
- Max. Pressure (BAR): 180
- Bucket Breakout Force (N): 24,500
- Reach (A): 1985 mm
- Digging Depth (B): 2640 mm
- Height to Pivot Pin (C): 2985 mm
- Overall Length (D): 3685 mm
- Ground Clearance (E): 205 mm
- Bucket Sizes: 300 / 450 / 600 mm
- Ditch Cleaning Bucket: 600 mm

### Sweeper Collector Attachment

A skid steer-mounted sweeper attachment for surface cleaning and debris collection.

**Technical Specification:**

- Weight: 405 kg
- Broom RPM: 55 ltr/min
